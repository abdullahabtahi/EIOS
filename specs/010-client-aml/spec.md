## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - High-Volume Alert Triage & False Positive Reduction (Priority: P1) 🎯 MVP

As an **Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance Officer**,  
I want the system to automatically triage and prioritize incoming transaction alerts down to a manageable queue of high-confidence cases,  
So that I spend my investigation time on genuine financial crime rather than burning out closing 95% false positives manually `[BRIEF monitoring]`.

**Why this priority**: Financial crime teams are drowning under ~2,000 weekly transaction alerts generated by static rules `[BRIEF monitoring]`. Triage scoring that filters 2,000 alerts down to $\le 50$ high-confidence cases `[BRIEF monitoring]` while preserving true positives is the single highest-leverage operational capability.

**Independent Test**: Ingest a simulated stream of 2,000 historical alerts containing planted money laundering cases, false alarms, and noisy thresholds; verify the triage scorer ranks them such that the top 50 cases capture $\ge 90\%$ of true laundering events `[TARGET]` while beating the static rule baseline by $\ge 0.15$ PR-AUC on sealed holdout evaluation sets `[CONSTITUTION Principle VI]`.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a weekly queue of 2,000 rule-triggered alerts with an ambient 95% false-positive rate `[BRIEF monitoring]`,  
   **When** the alert triage scorer processes the queue,  
   **Then** the system surfaces an executive worklist of $\le 50$ high-confidence cases `[BRIEF monitoring]` retaining $\ge 90\%$ of planted true positive laundering cases `[TARGET]`.
2. **Given** legitimate high-volume retail traders executing rapid, frequent transactions within their known wealth profile,  
   **When** evaluated by the alert triage engine,  
   **Then** the system suppresses false alarms, achieving at least a 30% relative reduction in false positives compared to naive static threshold baselines `[TARGET]`.

### User Story 2 - Real-Time Pre-Withdrawal Fraud Intervention (Priority: P1)

As a **Financial Crime Operations Lead**,  
I want the system to evaluate withdrawal requests in real time before funds leave the platform and propose a hold if money laundering risk is detected,  
So that illicit funds are secured before the account is abandoned `[BRIEF monitoring]`.

**Why this priority**: Once an illicit withdrawal is disbursed, funds are unrecoverable and the account is discarded `[BRIEF monitoring]`. Intervening before fund transmission turns reactive post-mortem audits into proactive asset protection.

**Independent Test**: Simulate inbound withdrawal requests across clean accounts, rapid round-trip accounts, and newly approved accounts; verify the pre-withdrawal sentinel evaluates risk within 2.0 seconds `[TARGET]`, emitting a cryptographically signed `HoldWithdrawal` proposal for high-risk transactions while allowing legitimate withdrawals to proceed without disruption.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** an account exhibiting acute laundering indicators requesting a withdrawal of customer balance,  
   **When** the pre-withdrawal evaluation executes,  
   **Then** the system evaluates transaction risk within 2.0 seconds (p95) `[TARGET]` and emits a Cloud KMS ECDSA P-256 signed `HoldWithdrawal` proposal placed in `PENDING` status for human review `[CONSTITUTION Principle II]`.
2. **Given** a high-value withdrawal proposal placed on hold,  
   **When** an compliance officer reviews the case in the approval interface,  
   **Then** the system enforces required human sign-off (with four-eyes dual authorization if exceeding policy threshold) before any balance action is confirmed `[CONSTITUTION Principle II]`.
3. **Given** a legitimate customer requesting a routine withdrawal conforming to their historical volume profile,  
   **When** evaluated by the sentinel,  
   **Then** the evaluation returns clean status without emitting a hold proposal, enabling frictionless straight-through processing.

### User Story 3 - Rapid Round-Trip Structuring & Micro-P&L Laundering Detection (Priority: P1)

As a **Senior AML Investigator**,  
I want the system to detect customers who deposit funds, execute brief minimal-risk trades with near-zero P&L, and promptly request withdrawals,  
So that we catch layering and money laundering cycles that bypass standard value-threshold rules `[BRIEF monitoring]`.

**Why this priority**: Criminals launder money by depositing funds, making token trades (e.g., $500 deposit, 10 minutes of trading with $0.01 profit `[BRIEF monitoring]`), and withdrawing clean broker funds. Traditional static threshold rules miss this because the transaction amounts look standard.

**Independent Test**: Provide transaction logs with planted sequence patterns (deposit $\to$ short-hold micro-P&L trade $\to$ withdrawal within 72 hours of KYC approval `[BRIEF monitoring]`); verify the sequence detector flags these round-trip typologies with exact turnover velocity and P&L symmetry calculations, beating static rules on sealed holdouts.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a customer who deposits $500, executes 10 minutes of trades yielding $|\text{net P&L}| \le \$0.05$, and requests a full withdrawal within 72 hours of KYC approval `[BRIEF monitoring]`,  
   **When** the round-trip sequence detector scans the account lifecycle,  
   **Then** the account is flagged with `ROUND_TRIP_STRUCTURING` and assigned an elevated typology risk score $\ge 0.85$.
2. **Given** an active retail scalper who trades with short holding durations but generates authentic market variance, directional exposure, and regular turnover,  
   **When** evaluated by the round-trip detector,  
   **Then** the system distinguishes genuine scalping from zero-risk structuring, avoiding false-positive alarms.

### User Story 4 - Smurfing & Mule Network Graph Clustering with Hub Suppression (Priority: P2)

As an **Intelligence & Network Analyst**,  
I want the system to identify coordinated money mule rings and smurfing clusters sharing payment credentials, devices, hardware fingerprints, or execution timing,  
So that organized syndicate networks are uncovered as cohesive clusters rather than isolated accounts `[BRIEF monitoring]`.

**Why this priority**: Sophisticated criminals deploy dozens of linked mule accounts to break large sums into small smurfed amounts `[BRIEF monitoring]`. Graph analysis reveals coordinated syndicate structures invisible to per-account rules.

**Independent Test**: Load a transaction and device network containing planted mule rings (e.g. 15 accounts sharing payment cards and hardware hashes) connected alongside high-degree public hubs (e.g. cafe Wi-Fi or CGNAT gateway); verify the graph engine isolates the illicit mule ring while suppressing legitimate public hub connections.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a network of 12 retail accounts sharing 2 payment methods, 3 hardware device IDs, and synchronized funding bursts,  
   **When** the mule ring clustering algorithm executes over the entity graph,  
   **Then** the system groups the accounts into a unified `MuleRingCluster` and identifies the core funding nodes with network degree and cross-account volume summaries.
2. **Given** hundreds of unrelated legitimate users sharing a high-traffic public Wi-Fi gateway or mobile carrier IP,  
   **When** the graph engine runs degree-penalized hub suppression,  
   **Then** the system suppresses the high-degree hub and avoids falsely merging innocent accounts into a single ring (hub false-merge rate $< 5\%$) `[TARGET]`.

### User Story 5 - Cold-Start Peer-Cohort Baselines vs. Mature Account Anomaly Detection (Priority: P2)

As a **Risk Operations Specialist**,  
I want the system to evaluate new accounts with zero historical activity against peer-cohort baselines (segment $\times$ tenure $\times$ country/product) and smoothly transition to personalized anomaly baselines as tenure grows,  
So that we solve the cold-start problem without generating massive false alarms on new signups `[BRIEF monitoring]`.

**Why this priority**: New customers lack personal history for deviation testing. Without peer-group normalization, new signups either escape monitoring completely or trip false alarms on their first legitimate deposit.

**Independent Test**: Evaluate transaction streams for both day-1 new accounts and 2-year veteran accounts; verify new accounts are benchmarked against statistical peer distributions, and veteran accounts are judged against robust per-customer deviation metrics.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a newly onboarded customer (tenure $< 7$ days) making a deposit that exceeds peer cohort 99th percentile by $4.0\sigma$,  
   **When** the peer-baseline anomaly detector evaluates the event,  
   **Then** the system flags an anomalous cold-start deviation based on peer-group distribution.
2. **Given** a mature, established customer with a 12-month track record of large regular deposits,  
   **When** they execute a transaction conforming to their personal baseline but exceeding general peer averages,  
   **Then** the system evaluates their personal historical distribution and suppresses false alerts.

As a **Compliance Officer / Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)**,

**Why this priority**: Manually pulling trades, KYC records, deposits, IP logs, and network graphs across 5+ disconnected systems takes hours per case `[BRIEF monitoring]`. Automated dossier aggregation and narrative drafting directly alleviates compliance burnout.

**Independent Test**: Query the system for an identified case; verify it generates a comprehensive investigation dossier and a regulator-ready SAR narrative (FinCEN / FIU standard format) where 100% of figures carry provenance citations `[CONSTITUTION Principle I]` and statutory references are cleanly normalized `[CONSTITUTION Principle VII]`.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** an investigation case involving a confirmed smurfing ring and round-trip transactions,  
   **When** the compliance officer requests a SAR filing draft,  
   **Then** the system compiles all relevant KYC events, transaction logs, trading records, and network connections into a formatted SAR narrative draft in $< 2.5$ seconds `[TARGET]`.
2. **Given** a generated SAR draft containing transaction volumes, dates, and statutory regulatory legal codes (e.g. `31 CFR 1010.410`, `31 U.S.C. 5318(g)`),  
   **When** validated against the Metric Auditor Gate,  
   **Then** 100% of business metrics cite verified data tools `[CONSTITUTION Principle I]` and statutory legal citations are normalized to prevent false hallucination rejection `[CONSTITUTION Principle VII]`.

### User Story 7 - Analyst Decision Feedback Loop & Novel Typology Mining (Priority: P3)

As a **Financial Crime Data Scientist**,  
I want the system to continuously ingest analyst investigation dispositions (Confirmed SAR vs. Dismissed False Alarm) and run unsupervised novelty mining on residual unexplained transactions,  
So that triage thresholds adapt to analyst decisions and discover zero-day laundering typologies `[BRIEF monitoring]`.

**Why this priority**: Compliance environments are adversarial. Static rules decay rapidly as bad actors evolve techniques. Ingesting analyst decisions and discovering novel typologies keeps detection ahead of criminals.

**Independent Test**: Feed historical analyst dispositions to the feedback loop and evaluate model recalibration; evaluate the unsupervised miner on sealed holdout datasets containing unplanted novel laundering structures; verify novel typologies are surfaced in the top-k novelty report.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a batch of 100 analyst investigation decisions (e.g. 20 SARs confirmed, 80 false alarms dismissed),  
   **When** the feedback loop processes the labeled batch,  
   **Then** the alert scoring thresholds recalibrate, demonstrating measurable improvement in precision@50 in subsequent evaluation rounds `[TARGET]`.
2. **Given** a sealed synthetic dataset containing a novel, unlabelled money laundering typology (e.g., cross-asset wash layering across 5 instruments),  
   **When** the unsupervised emerging pattern miner executes on unexplained residual activity,  
   **Then** the system flags the cluster in the top-5 candidate novelty list with an explainable behavioral profile `[TARGET]`.

### Edge Cases

- **Legitimate High-Net-Worth Scalpers**: High-frequency traders executing hundreds of rapid daily transactions must be distinguished from micro-P&L round-trip launderers using lifetime P&L variance, market risk exposure, and fee contribution metrics.
- **Shared IP / Public Wi-Fi Networks**: University campuses, shared offices, airport lounges, and carrier-grade NAT (CGNAT) gateways with thousands of shared users must be filtered via degree-penalized hub suppression to prevent spurious smurfing ring formation.
- **Cold-Start Brand New Accounts**: Customers with $< 3$ days tenure and no prior transactions default to peer-cohort baseline metrics; no individual z-score is calculated until minimum transaction count ($\ge 5$) is reached.
- **Statutory Law Citations in Text**: Mentions of legal codes (e.g. `31 CFR 1010`, `FinCEN Form 111`, `BSA Section 314(b)`) must not be confused with ungrounded business metrics by the Metric Auditor Gate.
- **Provider LLM Outages**: When external generative models are offline, the conversational specialist and SAR drafting tools MUST fallback to deterministic templated summaries marked with `[REPLAY]` badges `[CONSTITUTION Principle VIII]`.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST compute peer-group behavioral baselines segmented by client cohort, account tenure, and geographic product tier to solve cold-start anomaly scoring `[BRIEF monitoring]`.
- **FR-002**: System MUST calculate robust per-customer behavioral anomaly scores (deposit velocity, withdrawal frequency, volume deviation) for accounts with established history.
- **FR-003**: System MUST execute a supervised alert triage scorer ranking incoming alert streams, compressing $\ge 2,000$ raw alerts into $\le 50$ high-confidence investigation cases `[BRIEF monitoring]`.
- **FR-004**: System MUST implement a pre-registered naive rule-based baseline ("deposit $> \$10,000$" or "withdrawal count $> 3$ within 24h") and beat it by $\ge 0.15$ PR-AUC on sealed holdout evaluation sets `[CONSTITUTION Principle VI]`.
- **FR-005**: System MUST detect round-trip structuring sequences consisting of rapid deposits, micro-holding duration trades with near-zero P&L ($|\text{net P&L}| \le \$0.05$), and rapid withdrawal requests within 72 hours of KYC approval `[BRIEF monitoring]`.
- **FR-006**: System MUST construct and analyze entity relationship graphs across client accounts, shared payment tokens, device fingerprints, and external IP addresses `[BRIEF monitoring]`.
- **FR-007**: System MUST apply degree-penalized hub suppression to network graph nodes to suppress public Wi-Fi and CGNAT gateways, maintaining a hub false-merge rate $< 5\%$ `[TARGET]`.
- **FR-008**: System MUST evaluate withdrawal transactions in real time prior to fund disbursement, completing risk evaluation in $< 2.0$ seconds (p95) `[TARGET]`.
- **FR-009**: System MUST emit Cloud KMS ECDSA P-256 signed `ActionIntentToken` proposals for any automated risk mitigation (`HoldWithdrawal`, `FreezeAccount`) `[CONSTITUTION Principle II]`.
- **FR-010**: System MUST NOT execute any autonomous write-backs; all balance holds and account freezing proposals MUST require authenticated human approval (with four-eyes authorization for high severity) `[CONSTITUTION Principle II]`.
- **FR-011**: System MUST assemble comprehensive investigation dossiers collapsing 5+ source lookups (KYC status, deposit/withdrawal history, trade logs, device graph, compliance history) into a unified case record in $< 2.5$ seconds `[TARGET]`.
- **FR-012**: System MUST generate regulator-ready Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) narrative drafts complying with standard regulatory disclosure sections (Suspect Information, Summary of Suspicious Activity, Chronological Transaction Log, Reason for Suspicion).
- **FR-013**: System MUST guarantee 100% citation coverage for all financial and quantitative assertions in investigation dossiers and SAR drafts, with zero LLM-invented numbers `[CONSTITUTION Principle I]`.
- **FR-014**: System MUST normalize statutory regulatory citations (e.g. `31 CFR 1010`, `31 U.S.C. 5318(g)`) against a registered statutory taxonomy so the Metric Auditor Gate does not flag legal codes as ungrounded metrics `[CONSTITUTION Principle VII]`.
- **FR-015**: System MUST ingest analyst investigation dispositions (Confirmed SAR, Dismissed False Positive) to update training sets and recalibrate triage scoring thresholds.
- **FR-016**: System MUST execute unsupervised anomaly clustering on unexplained residual transactions to discover novel emerging laundering typologies.
- **FR-017**: System MUST provide plain-language, non-technical network relationship explanations in the `SentinelAMLSpecialist` conversational agent for non-technical compliance officers `[BRIEF monitoring]`.
- **FR-018**: System MUST support deterministic replay fallback with `[REPLAY]` badging during external LLM outages `[CONSTITUTION Principle VIII]`.

### Key Entities

- **`AMLAlert`**: The fundamental surveillance event containing alert ID, client ID, trigger rule/model, risk score, typology type, triggered timestamp, and raw alert attributes.
- **`AMLCaseDossier`**: The unified investigation pack aggregating client KYC profile, transaction history, trade summaries, network connections, prior alerts, and recommended disposition.
- **`RoundTripTypology`**: Identifies zero-risk wash/structuring behavior, capturing deposit amount, trade holding duration, net trading P&L, withdrawal request amount, and KYC approval recency delta.
- **`MuleRingCluster`**: Represents a coordinated group of accounts linked by shared payment credentials, hardware device IDs, or synchronized transaction timing, with master funding nodes and graph centrality metrics.
- **`WithdrawalRiskAssessment`**: Real-time evaluation record for an active withdrawal request, containing withdrawal ID, client ID, amount, assessed risk score, decision recommendation (`ALLOW` vs. `PROPOSE_HOLD`), and latency timing.
- **`SARDraft`**: Regulator-ready disclosure document containing filing metadata, subject identification, narrative summary, chronological transaction table, statutory citations, and provenance references.
- **`ActionIntentToken`**: Cryptographically signed proposal containing intent ID, action type (`HoldWithdrawal`, `FreezeAccount`), parameters, severity, evidence refs, nonce, expiry, and human signature fields.

## Assumptions

- **Hot State Data Availability**: Upstream L1 data products `transactions_aml`, `trading_activity`, `device_network_graph`, and `compliance_cases` are loaded and queryable in the Hot State engine.
- **Identity Privacy**: All client accounts, hardware device IDs, and payment methods have been tokenized with consistent deterministic cluster keys (`tok_id_cluster_<hex16>`) before ingestion into the AML module.
- **KMS Key Availability**: The Cloud KMS ECDSA P-256 cryptographic provider (or simulated local key provider in test environments) is initialized and active for signing action intents.
- **Approval Workflow**: A downstream human approval state machine exists to ingest, display, and execute `HoldWithdrawal` and `FreezeAccount` action intent proposals once approved.
- **SAR Jurisdiction Template**: SAR narrative structure follows FinCEN / standard international FIU disclosure conventions, with jurisdiction-specific templates configurable via parameters.
